Tap an animal, then tap a square — or drag it straight across. It lands when you let go, so slide off the board to change your mind. A wrong square costs a strike; five and the level starts over. Pick up the marker to cross out squares you have ruled out; tap one again to clear it. Your crosses clear each time an animal lands. M picks up the marker · Esc puts down what you hold · N goes to the next level once this one is done.

The key

Three colours, and three animals in every deal — usually one of each, sometimes two of one. An animal may only be settled in a land of its own colour, and a land takes one animal and no more — so as the board fills, the places a colour can still go run out. When a deal brings two of one colour, they need two different lands: pin one to a land and the other can't go there.

Every clue, and exactly what it means

This is the whole list — an animal never says anything else. Every deal can be solved from its three cards by crossing off squares until one is left for each animal: no guessing, and no "suppose it were here". My land is the coloured area an animal stands in, inside the heavy lines.

Options

A deal has exactly one arrangement its clues allow, and all three cards are on the table before anything is placed — so every square can be ruled in or out before you commit. A wrong square costs one of five strikes and the animal stays in hand. The fifth sends you back to the start of the same board.

The marker is for your own working: with it in hand, tap a square to cross it out and tap it again to clear it, or drag to do a row at once. Your crosses are the bold ones. The faint ones are the board's — a land with its animal is finished — and those stay. Yours are wiped each time an animal lands, because a cross doesn't say which animal it ruled out.

Squares of the wrong colour, or in a land that already has its animal, are not wrong answers: the board greys them out, and dropping on one costs nothing.

Showing the answer ends the level without finishing it — it stays unticked in the level list.

An animal only ever lands on its own square, so once it is down it is locked — there is nothing to undo.

Levels

    Every level is open — play them in order or jump ahead. They start small, with every animal placeable from its own card, and grow from there. A tick marks the ones you've finished.

Zoodoku

Settle the animals
Every land settled Each animal exactly where its own words put it.
— —

Tap an animal, then tap a square — or drag it straight across. Only its own colour will take it, and only where no animal has settled yet. Esc puts it down · U undoes · N deals a new board.

The key

Three lands, three colours, and one animal from each in every deal. An animal may only be settled in a land of its own colour, and a land takes one animal and no more — so as the board fills, the places a colour can still go run out.

What the animals mean

touch
The two squares share an edge. Corners do not count — an animal that means corners says corner to corner.
steps
Moves up, down, left or right between the two squares. A diagonal neighbour is two steps away.
my land
The coloured area an animal is standing in, not the colour itself. Two lands border each other when some square of one shares an edge with some square of the other.
halves
On a board with an odd number of rows, the middle row belongs to neither half — and likewise the middle column.

Options

A deal has exactly one arrangement its clues allow, so the moment your three sit consistently they are right, and the board settles them itself. There is nothing to submit and no way to be wrongly told you were right.

Gentle sticks to plain facts and throws in a clue more than the deal strictly needs. Sharp tells you the bare minimum: take one clue away and the deal would have two answers.

Settled animals are locked, because they were proved rather than guessed. Clearing a deal only clears the one you are on.
